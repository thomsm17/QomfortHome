Use the white-background QomfortHome logo on a subtle white backing in a translucent, blurred header that overlays the hero video; keep the logo on a white backing in the footer. The homepage hero should use a full-width aerial video of houses, not a square clip with side bars, with a sweeping #325129 shape over the lower-right area. Keep hero text and controls above that shape. Use the logo-sampled green #4c6b35 for text on light backgrounds and “goed voelt” (without glow). Use the darker sampled #325129 for primary buttons and the contact panel, with light text for contrast. Keep the FAQ section separate from the contact form and let the footer transition smoothly from the FAQ background into deeper green. Keep an accessible play/pause control, omit the eyebrow tagline and red rule above the headline, and use a relevant image for each process step.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a translucent top bar over the homepage hero while retaining the logo's white backing, a brand-green sweep over the drone video, separate FAQ and contact sections, and smooth transitions between the page sections; they also requested full-width drone footage, process-step images, and removal of the hero tagline and red rule.

**How to apply:** When changing the homepage hero or header, preserve these choices. Crop or regenerate media to a true landscape composition so the footage itself fills the hero without pillarboxing, and keep the decorative green shape behind readable hero content.
