Use the white-background QomfortHome logo in a white header and footer backing. The homepage hero should use a full-width aerial video of houses, not a square clip with side bars. Use the logo-sampled green #4c6b35 for text on light backgrounds and “goed voelt” (without glow). Use the darker sampled #325129 for primary buttons and the “Even overleggen?” panel, with light button/panel text for contrast; blend the footer from this panel green into its deeper green rather than using a hard edge. Keep an accessible play/pause control, omit the eyebrow tagline and red rule above the headline, use a relevant image for each process step, and keep FAQs in the “Even overleggen?” section.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the white logo/header treatment, a sampled green for text and a separate darker sample for primary buttons and the FAQ/contact panel, with a smoother transition into the footer; they also requested full-width drone footage, process-step images, FAQs grouped under “Even overleggen?”, and removal of the hero tagline and red rule.

**How to apply:** When changing the homepage hero or header, preserve these choices. Crop or regenerate media to a true landscape composition so the footage itself fills the hero without pillarboxing.
