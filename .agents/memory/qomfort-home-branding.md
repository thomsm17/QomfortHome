Use the white-background QomfortHome logo in a white header and footer backing. The homepage hero should use a full-width aerial video of houses, not a square clip with side bars. Use the logo-sampled green #4c6b35 for text on light backgrounds and for “goed voelt”, without a glow; retain light text on dark backgrounds for contrast. Match the video control to the “Bel ons” green. Keep an accessible play/pause control, omit the eyebrow tagline and red rule above the headline, use a relevant image for each process step, and keep FAQs in the “Even overleggen?” section.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the white logo/header treatment, a logo-sampled green for text without the hero glow, full-width drone footage, process-step images, and FAQs grouped under “Even overleggen?”; they also asked to remove the hero tagline and its red rule.

**How to apply:** When changing the homepage hero or header, preserve these choices. Crop or regenerate media to a true landscape composition so the footage itself fills the hero without pillarboxing.
