Use the white-background QomfortHome logo in a white header and footer backing. The homepage hero should use a full-width aerial video of houses, not a square clip with side bars. Keep an accessible play/pause control, use green accents for headline and icon colors, and omit the eyebrow tagline and red rule above the headline.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the white logo/header treatment and green accents, repeatedly asked for full-width drone footage, and asked to remove the hero tagline and its red rule.

**How to apply:** When changing the homepage hero or header, preserve these choices. Crop or regenerate media to a true landscape composition so the footage itself fills the hero without pillarboxing.
